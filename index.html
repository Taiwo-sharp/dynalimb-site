Our story

Born from experience
Driven by better care

Our founders had close personal connections with people living with limb loss. They saw the physical, emotional and financial strain on individuals and families: limited access to specialized care, high costs and long, slow processes.

This inspired Dynalimb: a company using 3D technology, clinical expertise and local manufacturing to make prosthetic and orthotic care more accessible.

Today, we’re building better ways to design, produce and deliver mobility solutions across Africa.
